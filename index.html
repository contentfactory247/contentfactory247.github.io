ChapterEk Uploader

A private publishing tool for the ChapterEk YouTube channel. Uses YouTube API Services.

ChapterEk on YouTube · youtube.com/@chapter_ek

ChapterEk makes Hindi story videos about money and life lessons from books. ChapterEk Uploader is the small in-house tool that uploads those videos, with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, to ChapterEk's own YouTube channel and schedules them.

Who uses it

Only the ChapterEk channel owner, signed in with ChapterEk's own Google account. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads a finished, owner-approved video to the ChapterEk channel and sets its publish time.
  • Sets the video's custom thumbnail.
  • Reads the channel's name to confirm it is connected.
